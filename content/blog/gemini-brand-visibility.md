**Gemini often grounds its answers in Google Search, so the brands it names for "best" and "alternatives" questions largely come from pages that rank in Google.** Getting mentioned in Gemini is therefore mostly Google SEO plus appearing in the third-party pages Google ranks for your buyers' questions. For questions it answers from memory, it reflects how widely the web described your brand before its training cutoff.

## What to work on

1. **Be indexed and rank in Google** for the questions buyers ask. Check Search Console for indexing problems first.
2. **Appear on the pages that rank**: listicles, review sites, comparison pages, forums. Ask Gemini your questions and open the sources it shows.
3. **Answer questions directly on your own pages**: a one-sentence answer, then a table and specifics.
4. **Keep your brand description consistent** across your site, profiles and directories.
5. **Check Google-Extended before blocking it.** Google uses it to control training and grounding in Gemini and other Google systems, so blocking it can reduce how Gemini uses your pages. Blocking Googlebot removes you from Search itself.

## How to check

Ask Gemini the same buyer questions several times, note who is named and which sources are linked. Seenlense tracks Gemini weekly on every plan, alongside ChatGPT Search and Perplexity, and separates mentions from recommendations (Seenlense is our product).

## Gemini vs Google AI Overviews

Both draw on Google Search, but they are different products with different answers. Being named in one does not guarantee the other, so check both. See [AI Overviews and AI Mode](/blog/google-ai-overviews-brand-visibility).

## FAQ

### Does Gemini use Google Search?

Gemini can ground answers in Google Search results, and often does for current or product questions, showing the sources it used.

### Why does Gemini give different brands from ChatGPT?

They search different indexes and use different models. Track each engine separately.
