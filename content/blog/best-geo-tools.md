## 2. Access: can AI read your site?

- **Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools** (free): if a page is not indexed, Gemini, Google's AI features and Bing-based answers cannot use it. Google states there are no extra requirements for AI Overviews beyond being indexed and eligible for a snippet.
- **Your robots.txt**: allow OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User. See [robots.txt for AI crawlers](/blog/robots-txt-for-ai-crawlers).
- **View source**: your key content should be in the HTML, not only rendered by JavaScript.

## 3. Sources: what does AI trust in your category?

The pages AI cites when answering your buyers' questions are the GEO equivalent of a backlink list. Trackers that record cited sources (Seenlense, Peec AI, Otterly.AI, Profound and others) show which review sites, listicles, forums and docs you need to appear on. See [AI citation tracking](/blog/ai-citation-tracking).

## FAQ

### What is a GEO tool?

Software that helps a brand appear in answers from generative AI such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, mainly by measuring visibility and showing what to change.

### Is GEO different from SEO?

It builds on SEO. AI assistants that search the web read pages that rank, so SEO basics still matter; GEO adds tracking AI answers and earning mentions on the sources AI cites. See [GEO vs SEO vs AEO](/blog/geo-vs-seo-vs-aeo).

### Do I need a paid GEO tool?

Start with a free check. Pay for tracking once you are making changes and need to see whether they work.
