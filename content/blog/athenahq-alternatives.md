**AthenaHQ has a free Essential tier (300 credits on 5 engines) and a Starter plan at $295 a month for 3,600 credits across 11 models; Enterprise is custom.** One credit is one AI response. If the free tier is too small and $295 too much, Seenlense, Otterly.AI, LLMrefs and Peec AI sit in between.

Details were checked on AthenaHQ's pricing page on November 14, 2026. Seenlense is our product.

## AthenaHQ plans

| | Essential | Starter | Enterprise |

| Credits | 300 | 3,600 | Custom |

API access and extra credits are paid add-ons on Starter.

## What 3,600 credits buys

At one credit per answer, 3,600 credits is 50 prompts on 6 engines, run 12 times a month, or 100 prompts on 9 engines run 4 times. Plan your prompt list first, then check it fits.
