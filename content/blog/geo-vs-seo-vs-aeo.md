**SEO (search engine optimization) gets your pages ranked in search results; AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your content used as the direct answer; GEO (generative engine optimization) gets your brand named and cited in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI features.** They overlap heavily: AI assistants that search the web read pages that rank, so SEO is the base for both.

## Side by side

| | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank in results | Be the direct answer | Be named and cited by AI |
| Where | Google, Bing results | Featured snippets, voice, AI Overviews | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, AI Mode |
| Main levers | Relevance, links, technical health | Clear question-and-answer content, structure | Ranking pages + mentions on cited sources + consistent brand description |
| Measured by | Rankings, clicks | Snippet ownership | Mention, recommendation and citation rates |

## What is genuinely different about GEO

- **Third-party pages matter more.** For "best X" questions, AI often summarises listicles and reviews, so being on them can matter more than your own page.
- **No fixed rank.** Answers vary per run, so you measure rates over many runs. See [what AI search visibility is](/blog/what-is-ai-search-visibility).
- **Recommendation vs mention.** Being named is not the same as being chosen.
- **Training data.** Models answering from memory reflect how the web described you before their cutoff.

## What to do first

1. Fix SEO basics: indexing, crawl access, fast pages.
2. Write answer-first pages for your buyers' questions (helps AEO and GEO).
3. Measure AI answers for those questions and find the cited sources.
4. Get onto those sources.

## FAQ

### Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO, because AI assistants that search the web rely on pages that rank.

### Is AEO the same as GEO?

They overlap. AEO is older and focused on direct answers in search; GEO focuses on generative AI assistants and whether they name your brand.
