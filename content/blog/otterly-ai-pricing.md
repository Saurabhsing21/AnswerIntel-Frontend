## How credits work

Otterly's FAQ explains it simply: **one credit is one prompt checked once on one engine.** A prompt tracked daily on one engine uses 31 credits a month. Claude costs 3 credits per answer because it is more expensive for Otterly to run.

So Lite's 2,000 credits cover 20 prompts × 3 engines × 31 days = 1,860 credits, with a little room to spare. If one of your three engines is Claude, the same 20 prompts need about 3,100 credits, more than Lite includes.

## Our recommendation

Otterly.AI Lite is the cheapest way to get **daily** data on a short list of questions, and it covers engines several cheaper tools don't (AI Overviews, AI Mode, Copilot). Pick it if 20 questions really cover your market. If you need 50 to 150 questions and can live with weekly data, a weekly tracker will cost a fraction of Standard.

## Common mistakes

1. **Adding Claude without checking credits.** At 3 credits an answer, it can push you over Lite's 2,000.
2. **Tracking 20 branded prompts.** Use the slots for unbranded category and competitor questions.
3. **Jumping to Standard for prompt count alone.** If you don't need daily data or the API, compare weekly trackers first.
