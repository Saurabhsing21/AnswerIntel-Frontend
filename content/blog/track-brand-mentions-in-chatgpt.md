**To track brand mentions in ChatGPT, ask it the questions your buyers ask, many times, and count how often it names and recommends you.** OpenAI gives brands no analytics, so the only way to measure is to sample answers yourself or with a tool that does it on a schedule.

## Step 1: Write the questions your buyers ask

Aim for 30 to 50, in three groups:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Category | "Best CRM for a 10-person startup" |
| Comparison | "HubSpot vs Pipedrive for small teams" |
| Problem | "How do I stop losing leads in my inbox?" |

Use your buyers' words, not your marketing copy. Sales calls, support tickets and Reddit threads are good sources.

## Step 2: Ask in a clean session

Answers depend on memory and chat history. Use a logged-out or temporary chat, and turn memory off, so you see what a new buyer sees. ChatGPT also answers differently with and without web search, so track both if you can.

## Step 3: Record four things per answer

1. **Mentioned:** did it name you?
2. **Recommended:** did it tell the buyer to choose you?
3. **Position:** where were you in the list?
4. **Sources:** which pages did it cite, when it searched?

The gap between mentioned and recommended matters most. Many brands are named in passing while a competitor gets the recommendation.

## Step 4: Repeat, because answers vary

Ask the same question twice and you often get different lists. One answer tells you little. Ask each question several times, or on several days, and track the rate: mentions divided by answers. A move from 30% to 35% across 50 answers is usually noise. A move from 30% to 50% is probably real.

## FAQ

### Can I see how many times ChatGPT mentioned my brand?

Not directly. OpenAI does not share mention data. You can only sample answers and estimate a rate.

### Does ChatGPT give the same answer to everyone?

No. Answers vary between runs, and also with location, memory and whether web search is used.

### How often should I check?

Weekly is enough for most brands. Answers change as pages get indexed and models update.
