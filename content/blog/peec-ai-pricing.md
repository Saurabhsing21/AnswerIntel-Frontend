**Peec AI costs $95 a month for Starter (50 prompts), $245 for Pro (150 prompts) and $495 for Advanced (350 prompts). Every plan tracks 3 AI models of your choice, daily, with unlimited users.** A fourth model costs $30 a month on Starter, so the price most teams actually pay is higher than the headline. Enterprise is custom, with up to 26 models, API and SSO.

We read every number below from Peec AI's pricing page in a browser on November 14, 2026. Seenlense, which appears in the alternatives section, is our product.

## What it really costs: models are the catch

Each plan includes three models. Most buyers want at least ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, and many also want Google AI Overviews, which makes four. Peec prices an extra model by the prompts in your plan; on Starter it is $30 a month (or $60 a year less on annual billing).

| What you want to track | Starter cost |
|---|---|
| 3 models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) | $95/month |
| 4 models (+ AI Overviews) | $125/month |
| 5 models (+ AI Mode) | $155/month |

Per tracked prompt, Starter works out at $1.90 a month, Pro at $1.63 and Advanced at $1.41. Because every prompt runs daily on 3 models, Starter collects about 4,500 answers a month, or about $21 per 1,000 answers.

## How to sign up

1. Open [peec.ai/pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing) and choose **Get started** on a plan, or **Start free trial**.
2. Add your brand, website and competitors.
3. Pick your 3 models. Choose the engines your buyers use; for most B2B products that is ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini or AI Overviews.
4. Add prompts. Write them in your buyers' words, mostly unbranded: "best [category] for [audience]", "[competitor] alternatives".
5. Wait for the first daily runs before judging the numbers.

## Our recommendation

Peec AI is a strong product if you need **daily** data, **several countries** or **regional engines** like Naver and ERNIE, and a team of people looking at the same dashboard. For a startup that needs a baseline and a list of what to fix, $95 to $155 a month is more than you need to start: run a free check first, and move to Peec when you need daily reporting.

## Common mistakes

1. **Budgeting for $95 and needing four models.** Decide your engines first; the real price is often $125+.
2. **Spending 50 prompts on branded questions.** Most of them should be unbranded category and comparison questions.
3. **Reading single days.** Daily data is noisy. Look at weekly averages.
4. **Choosing models your buyers don't use.** Check where your audience actually asks.

## FAQ

### How much does Peec AI cost per month?

$95 for Starter (50 prompts), $245 for Pro (150) and $495 for Advanced (350), each with 3 models tracked daily. Enterprise is custom.

### Does Peec AI have a free plan?

No, but its pricing page offers a free trial.

### How much does an extra model cost on Peec AI?

$30 a month on Starter. Peec prices extra models by the number of prompts in your plan, so they cost more on Pro and Advanced.

### Does Peec AI have an API?

On Enterprise. Advanced includes a Looker Studio integration.
