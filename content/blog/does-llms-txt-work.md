**llms.txt is a proposed Markdown file at your site's root that summarises what your site is and links to its key pages for AI tools; it does not, on its own, make AI assistants recommend you.** Google says no AI text files are needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. It is cheap to add and harmless, so add one if you like, but spend most of your time elsewhere.

## What llms.txt is

A plain Markdown file at /llms.txt with your name, a one-paragraph summary, and links to important pages. You can see ours at [seenlense.com/llms.txt](/llms.txt).

## What it does not do

- It is not a ranking signal in Google. Google's AI features documentation says no new machine-readable files or AI text files are needed.
- It does not replace robots.txt. Crawlers still follow robots.txt for access.
- It does not get you onto the third-party pages that drive most recommendations.

## When it can help

- AI coding and agent tools that look for it to understand docs.
- A single, clean summary of your product, pricing and pages that any tool can read quickly.
- Keeping your brand description consistent in one place.

## What matters more

1. Being indexed in Google and Bing.
2. Allowing the AI search crawlers in robots.txt ([guide](/blog/robots-txt-for-ai-crawlers)).
3. Pages that answer buyer questions in the first sentence.
4. Mentions on the pages AI cites ([citation tracking](/blog/ai-citation-tracking)).

## FAQ

### Does Google use llms.txt?

Google's documentation for AI Overviews and AI Mode says no AI text files are needed to appear in them.

### Should I add llms.txt?

It takes ten minutes and does no harm, so it is fine to add. Do not expect it to change AI recommendations by itself.
