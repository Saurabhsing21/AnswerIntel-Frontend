**Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit costs $99 a month for one domain and 25 tracked prompts, and Semrush says a paid subscription is required to access it.** Extra domains are $99 each and 50 more prompts cost $60 a month. If you only need AI tracking, standalone tools like Seenlense, Rankscale, Otterly.AI, LLMrefs and Peec AI give more prompts for the money.

Details were checked on Semrush's pricing and knowledge-base pages on November 14, 2026. Seenlense is our product.
