**For agencies, the deciding numbers are projects (one per client), prompts per project, seats and exports, not the entry price.** LLMrefs (unlimited projects, $79), Peec AI (up to 5 projects on Advanced), Scrunch (5 user licenses on Core, plus a separate agency pricing tab), Profound (an Agency Growth plan) and Seenlense Pro (10 projects, $100) are the usual shortlist.

Prices were checked on each pricing page on November 14, 2026. Seenlense is our product.

## What matters for an agency

- **Price per client.** Divide the plan price by the clients it holds. Seenlense Pro works out at $10 per client across 10 projects; LLMrefs depends on how you split 500 prompts.
- **Prompts per client.** 20 to 50 good buyer questions per client is a workable start.
- **White-label reports and exports.** Check CSV export or an API if you build your own client reports (Otterly from Standard, Profound on Enterprise).
- **Explaining results.** Clients ask "why is the competitor recommended?". Tools that show cited sources and competitor wins make that conversation easier.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI visibility tool for agencies?

For many small clients, LLMrefs or Seenlense Pro keep the per-client cost low. For agencies serving larger brands with daily reporting, Peec AI Advanced or Scrunch.

### How many prompts per client?

Start with 20 to 50 per client: category questions, competitor comparisons and problem questions. Add more once you know which ones move.
