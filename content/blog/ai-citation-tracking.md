**AI citation tracking records which web pages ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI features cite when they answer your buyers' questions.** Those pages are where the recommendation comes from. If a "best tools" article cites three of your competitors and not you, getting onto that page often matters more than editing your own site.

## Which engines show citations

| Engine | Shows sources? |
|---|---|
| Perplexity | Yes, on every answer |
| ChatGPT Search | Yes, when it searches the web |
| Gemini | Often, when it grounds the answer in Google Search |
| Google AI Overviews / AI Mode | Yes, as links in the answer |
| Claude | Yes, when web search is on |
| Models answering from memory | No |

## How to use citation data

1. **List the cited domains for your top 20 questions.** Group them: review sites, listicles, forums (Reddit), docs, news, competitors' own sites.
2. **Find the pages that cite competitors but not you.** These are your outreach list: ask to be added, offer data, or answer the forum thread.
3. **Check your own pages that get cited.** Keep them current; dated pricing and answer-first paragraphs get reused.
4. **Re-check after changes.** A new mention on a cited page usually shows up in live-search engines within weeks.

## Tools that record citations

Most AI visibility trackers list cited sources per answer, including Seenlense, Peec AI, Otterly.AI and Profound. Seenlense groups them per question so you can see which source each competitor's recommendation came from. You can also do it by hand in Perplexity, which always shows sources. ## FAQ

### What is an AI citation?

A link to a source page that an AI assistant shows with its answer, meaning it read that page to build the answer.

### Is a citation the same as a recommendation?

No. You can be cited as a source while a competitor is recommended, and you can be recommended because a third-party page you appear on was cited. Track both.

### How do I get cited by AI?

Rank for the question in normal search, answer it in the first sentence, keep facts dated and specific, and get mentioned on the pages AI already cites. See [Perplexity SEO](/blog/perplexity-seo).
