**Google says there are no extra requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode: a page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet in normal Search, and no special markup or AI text file is needed.** Which brands get named then depends on the pages Google's AI draws from, so the work is classic SEO plus being mentioned on the pages that rank for your buyers' questions.

Google's guidance quoted here is from its "AI features and your website" documentation, checked November 14, 2026.

## What Google says

- Pages need to be indexed and eligible for a snippet. There are no additional technical requirements.
- No new machine-readable files, AI text files or special schema are needed.
- Normal fundamentals apply: allow crawling, internal links, good page experience, structured data that matches visible text.
- Traffic from AI Overviews and AI Mode is counted in Search Console's Performance report under "Web", together with other Search traffic.
- You can limit what is shown with nosnippet, data-nosnippet, max-snippet or noindex.

## What influences which brands are named

1. **Ranking pages.** AI Overviews summarise pages Google already considers relevant. If listicles rank for "best [category]", the brands in those listicles are named.
2. **Direct answers.** Pages that answer the question in the first sentence, with specifics, are easier to quote.
3. **Entity clarity.** A consistent description of your brand across your site, Google Business Profile (if local), and profiles helps Google connect you to the category.
4. **Freshness on changing facts.** Dated prices and comparisons get preferred for "2026" and "pricing" questions.

## How to measure

Search Console does not separate AI Overview traffic. To see whether you are named, check the questions in Google yourself, or use a tracker that covers AI Overviews and AI Mode (Otterly.AI, Peec AI, Semrush, Ahrefs Brand Radar, Scrunch, AthenaHQ). Seenlense does not cover AI Overviews yet; it covers Gemini, which also uses Google Search (Seenlense is our product).

## FAQ

### Do I need special schema for AI Overviews?

No. Google says no special schema or AI text files are needed. Structured data should match what is visible on the page.

### Can I see AI Overview clicks in Search Console?

They are included in the Performance report under Web search, but not shown separately.

### Does llms.txt help with AI Overviews?

Google says AI text files are not needed. See [does llms.txt work](/blog/does-llms-txt-work).
