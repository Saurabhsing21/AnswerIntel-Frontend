**Choose Otterly.AI if you want daily tracking of a few prompts for under $30 a month. Choose Peec AI if you need 50+ prompts tracked daily with a more complete dashboard.** Both track ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers. They differ most in what you get at each price.

Prices were checked on both pricing pages on November 14, 2026.

## Side by side

| | Peec AI | Otterly.AI |
|---|---|---|
| Entry plan | Starter, $95/month | Lite, $29/month |
| Prompts on entry plan | 50 | 20 |
| Mid plan | Pro, $245 for 150 prompts | Standard, $189 for 160 prompts |
| Top self-serve plan | Advanced, $495 for 350 prompts | Premium, $489 for 535 prompts |
| How engines are paid | 3 models per plan, extra models from $30/month | Credits, 3 of 7 engines per plan |
| Engines available | ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overview, AI Mode, Gemini, Naver, ERNIE, Bing AI Mode | ChatGPT, AI Overviews, Perplexity, Copilot, AI Mode, Gemini, Claude |
| Frequency | Daily | Daily (Premium: daily or weekly) |
| Seats | Unlimited | Unlimited team members |
| API | Enterprise | Standard and up |
| Annual discount | Shown for extra models | 15% |

## Where Peec AI wins

- **More prompts at entry level:** 50 versus 20.
- **Regional engines:** Naver and ERNIE for Korea and China.
- **Country tracking:** 3 countries from Pro.

## Where Otterly.AI wins

- **Price to start:** $29 versus $95.
- **Claude and Copilot**, which Peec's standard plans don't list.
- **API from the mid plan**, which Peec keeps for Enterprise.

## FAQ

### Is Peec AI or Otterly.AI cheaper?

Otterly is cheaper to start ($29 versus $95). Per prompt at the mid tier Otterly is cheaper: Peec Pro is about $1.63 a prompt, and Otterly Standard about $1.18.

### Which tracks more AI engines?

Otterly lists 7 engines and Peec lists 8 on standard plans, but each plan lets you use only 3 (Peec) or 4 (Otterly) at a time without paying more.
