**Ahrefs Brand Radar tracks your brand across AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot and Claude, with the AI add-on starting at $199 a month and custom prompt packs from $50 a month, on top of an Ahrefs plan that starts at $129.** If you do not already use Ahrefs, standalone AI trackers cost a fraction of that.

Prices were checked on Ahrefs' pricing and Brand Radar pages on November 14, 2026 (excluding tax). Seenlense is our product.

## What Brand Radar costs

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Ahrefs Lite / Standard / Advanced | $129 / $249 / $449 a month |
| Brand Radar AI add-on | From $199/month |
| Custom prompt packs | Basic $50, Growth $100, Scale $250 a month |

Ahrefs says prompt tracking is available on paid plans from Lite up. Its strength is the large pre-built index of AI answers, which shows brand visibility across many prompts you did not write yourself.

## Standalone alternatives

| Tool | Price | Prompts | Engines |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seenlense | $20/month | 50, weekly | ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini |
| Rankscale | $17/month yearly | ~480 answers | 17+ |
| Otterly.AI | $29/month | 20, daily | 3 of 7, incl. AI Overviews and Copilot |
| LLMrefs | $79/month | 500, weekly | 11 |
| Peec AI | $95/month | 50, daily | 3 of 8, incl. AI Overviews |

If Google AI Overviews and Copilot matter most to you, Otterly.AI or Peec AI are the closest match. Seenlense does not track those two yet.

## When Brand Radar is worth it

- You already pay for Ahrefs.
- You want market-wide visibility across a big index of prompts, not just your own list.
- You want AI mentions next to backlinks and organic rankings.

## FAQ

### How much does Ahrefs Brand Radar cost?

The AI add-on starts at $199 a month, with custom prompt packs from $50 a month, on top of an Ahrefs plan from $129 a month (prices excluding tax, November 2026).

### Which AI engines does Brand Radar cover?

AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot, with Claude available.

### What is a cheaper Brand Radar alternative?

Seenlense ($20) or Rankscale ($17 yearly) for tracking your own prompts; Otterly.AI ($29) if you need AI Overviews.
