## What each free check is good for

- **Seenlense free check:** the closest to what a buyer sees, because both engines search the live web. It shows whether you are recommended or only mentioned, and who is recommended instead.
- **HubSpot's grader:** a quick read of how models describe your brand from what they learned in training. Good for brand perception, not for live recommendations.
- **Mangools' grader:** a visibility score against competitors across several models, including open models.
- **AthenaHQ Essential:** an ongoing free tier, limited by credits.
- **Trials:** the full paid workflow, but set a reminder before day 7.

## How to check by hand

1. Open ChatGPT (with search on), Perplexity and Gemini in a private window.
2. Ask 5 to 10 questions a buyer would ask: "best [category] for [audience]", "[competitor] alternatives", "[your problem] tool".
3. Ask each question 3 times. Answers vary, so count how often you are named, not whether you were named once.
4. Note which sites each answer cites. Those are the pages you need to be on.

Doing this every week is where a tracker starts to pay for itself. See [how to track brand mentions in ChatGPT](/blog/track-brand-mentions-in-chatgpt).

## FAQ

### Is there a free AI visibility tool?

Yes, as one-off checks: Seenlense's free check, HubSpot's AI Search Grader and Mangools' AI Search Grader are free. AthenaHQ has a free tier with limited credits. Ongoing weekly tracking is paid, from about $17 to $20 a month.

### Why do free checks give different results?

They ask different questions on different models, and some read live web results while others use training data. Answers also vary from run to run.

### How accurate is a single check?

It is a snapshot. Repeat the same questions a few times, or track weekly, before drawing conclusions.
