**To get recommended by ChatGPT, appear on the pages it reads when it searches (rankings, listicles, reviews, forums), make your own pages answer buyer questions in the first sentence, and keep your brand described the same way everywhere.** Search-based answers change within weeks; what the model learned in training changes only with new models. Here is the order to work in.

## 1. Measure where you stand

Ask ChatGPT, with search on, the 10 to 30 questions a buyer asks before choosing a product like yours. Ask each a few times. Note who is recommended and which sources are cited. A [free check](/check) does this for 5 questions in a minute.

## 2. Make sure ChatGPT can read your site

Allow OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User in robots.txt, keep key content in the HTML, and get indexed in Bing and Google. See [robots.txt for AI crawlers](/blog/robots-txt-for-ai-crawlers).

## 3. Say what you are in one sentence

"[Brand] is a [category] for [audience] that [main benefit]." Put it at the top of your homepage, About page, social profiles and directory listings. Consistent descriptions help both search and training data connect your brand to your category.

## 4. Build pages for the questions buyers ask

One page per important question: "best [category] for [audience]", "[competitor] alternatives", "[competitor] pricing", "[your brand] vs [competitor]", and how-to guides for the problem you solve. Answer in the first sentence, use a table, date your facts, and be honest about where competitors are better.

## 5. Get onto the pages ChatGPT already cites

This is usually the biggest lever. Look at the sources cited for your top questions and work through them: ask roundup authors to include you, get listed on review sites and directories, answer relevant Reddit and community threads, and publish original data others will quote.

## 6. Earn independent mentions over time

Product launches, podcasts, guest posts, customer case studies and comparison sites all add to how often the web describes you, which is what future models learn from.

## 7. Re-measure and test

Track the same questions weekly. When you ship a change, compare before and after across several runs, because single answers vary. See [how ChatGPT decides which brands to recommend](/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend) for the mechanics.

## FAQ

### How long does it take to get recommended by ChatGPT?

For answers where ChatGPT searches the web, weeks after the pages it reads change. For answers from memory, months, because that needs a new model.

### Can I pay to be recommended by ChatGPT?

There is no paid placement in ChatGPT's organic answers. You influence them through the pages ChatGPT reads and how widely your brand is mentioned.

### Why does ChatGPT recommend my competitor?

Usually because the competitor appears on more of the pages ChatGPT cites. See [why ChatGPT recommends competitors](/blog/why-chatgpt-recommends-competitors).
