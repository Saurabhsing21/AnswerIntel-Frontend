## What the money buys

**Prompts × engines × runs is the real size of a plan.** 50 prompts on 3 engines, run weekly, is about 650 answers a month. The same 50 prompts run daily on 3 engines is about 4,500. Daily data is smoother, but most teams make decisions weekly.

**Live search versus memory.** ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Gemini search the web before answering, so they react to new pages within weeks. Models that answer from memory change only when a new model ships. Check which kind each tool tracks.

**Insight, not just scores.** Ask whether the tool shows which pages the AI cited, separates being mentioned from being recommended, and tells you whether a change worked.

## FAQ

### What is the cheapest AI visibility tool?

Rankscale Essentials at $17 a month on yearly billing. On monthly billing, Seenlense Starter at $20.

### Is there a free AI visibility tool?

Most offer a trial or a free first scan rather than a free plan. Seenlense gives one free scan without a card, and LLMrefs has a 7-day trial.

### How many prompts do I need?

Start with 30 to 50 real buyer questions: category questions ("best X for Y"), comparisons with your top competitors, and problem questions your product solves.
