**Scrunch's self-serve plan, Core, costs $250 a month for 125 unique prompts, 5,000 responses a month, 1 brand workspace and 5 user licenses, tracking ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Copilot.** Enterprise is custom-priced and expands to 9 engines, API access and SSO. If $250 is more than you need, Seenlense, Rankscale, Otterly.AI, LLMrefs and Peec AI all start lower.

Prices were checked on Scrunch's pricing page in a browser on November 14, 2026. Seenlense is our product.

Seenlense Growth gives roughly the same prompt count as Scrunch Core for a fifth of the price, with Gemini and Claude instead of AI Overviews and Copilot.

## When Scrunch is the better fit

- You need Google AI Overviews and Copilot on the entry plan.
- Five people need their own logins.
- You want site audits and Scrunch's agent experience features on Enterprise.
