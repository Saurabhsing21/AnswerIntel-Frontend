**If you want Profound-style AI visibility tracking without a sales call, the main options are Peec AI, Otterly.AI, Rankscale, LLMrefs and Seenlense.** All five publish their prices and let you start on your own. Profound's pricing page now shows only a free 7-day trial and a custom-priced Enterprise plan for brands.

Prices below were checked on each vendor's pricing page on November 14, 2026. Seenlense is our product, so weigh that section accordingly.

## What changed at Profound

Through most of 2026, Profound sold two self-serve plans to brands, widely reported as Starter at $99 a month and Growth at $399 a month. Its pricing page now lists:

- **Trial:** free for 7 days, 50 prompts run daily on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, with no history or exports.
- **Enterprise:** custom pricing, up to 10 answer engines, API, SSO and dedicated support.
- **Agency Growth:** a self-serve plan for agencies, with no price shown on the page.

If you are a large brand with budget for an enterprise contract, Profound is still a strong product. If you are a startup or small team, you need something you can buy today.

## Peec AI

Peec AI is the closest like-for-like replacement: a polished dashboard, daily tracking, unlimited seats and models you choose. Starter is $95 a month for 50 prompts on 3 models and 1 country. Pro is $245 for 150 prompts, and Advanced is $495 for 350. Each extra model costs $30 a month on Starter.

**Best for:** marketing teams that want daily data and a Profound-like workflow at a published price.

## Otterly.AI

Otterly is the cheapest way to get daily tracking. Lite is €29 a month for 16 prompts on 4 of its 7 engines. Standard is €189 for 120 prompts and adds an API, and Premium is €489 for 403 prompts. Engines are paid in credits, and a Claude answer costs 3 credits.

**Best for:** watching a small, fixed set of questions every day.

## Rankscale

Rankscale is credit-based and covers more engines than anyone else here (17+). Essentials is $17 a month on yearly billing for 120 credits, or about 480 answers. Pro is $84 for 1,200 credits. Search terms are unlimited, and you choose how often each one runs.

**Best for:** people who want many engines and are comfortable budgeting in credits.

## LLMrefs

LLMrefs has one plan, $79 a month, covering 500 prompts on nine engines, including Claude, Grok, Copilot and Meta AI. It also includes geo-targeting in 50+ countries and unlimited projects. Reports refresh weekly.

**Best for:** agencies and teams tracking many prompts across many markets.

## Seenlense

Seenlense starts at $20 a month for 50 prompts, asked every week on ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Gemini. All three search the live web, the way a buyer would see them. Growth ($50) adds Claude and 120 prompts. Pro ($100) adds DeepSeek, Llama and Mistral and 200 prompts. Every account gets one free scan, no card needed.

What sets it apart is what happens after the numbers. It separates mentions from recommendations, shows which pages each AI cited, and runs before-and-after experiments that tell you whether a change beat normal run-to-run variation. It does not track Google AI Overviews or Copilot yet, and it tracks weekly, not daily.

**Best for:** founders who want to know what to fix, on a startup budget.

## How to choose

1. **Daily or weekly?** AI answers vary from run to run, so most small teams act on weekly trends. Pay for daily only if you report to someone who needs it.
2. **Which engines do your buyers use?** For most B2B products that is ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini first.
3. **Do you need "what to fix" or just "where we stand"?** Some tools stop at visibility scores.

## FAQ

### Did Profound remove its Starter plan?

Profound's pricing page for brands, checked November 14, 2026, lists only a free trial and custom Enterprise pricing. Starter and Growth are no longer shown.

### What is the cheapest Profound alternative?

Rankscale Essentials at $17 a month on yearly billing, then Seenlense Starter at $20 a month on monthly billing.

### Is there a free Profound alternative?

Seenlense gives every account one free scan without a card, and Profound itself offers a 7-day free trial. Ongoing tracking is paid with every tool here.
