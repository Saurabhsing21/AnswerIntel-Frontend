**As of November 2026, Profound does not publish a price for brands: its pricing page shows a free 7-day trial and an Enterprise plan with custom pricing.** The self-serve Starter and Growth plans, widely reported at $99 and $399 a month, are no longer listed. Agencies have a self-serve Agency Growth plan, but the page shows no price for it.

All details below come from Profound's pricing page, checked November 14, 2026.

## Profound plans today

| | Trial | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free for 7 days | Custom |
| Prompts | 50, run daily | Custom, daily |
| Answer engines | ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews | Up to 10, including Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Claude, DeepSeek and Qwen |
| History and exports | No | All-time history, CSV/JSON export, API |
| Languages and regions | 1 and 1 | Custom |
| Security | | SSO/SAML, SOC 2 |
| Seats | Unlimited | Unlimited |

The page also mentions **Agency Growth**, a self-serve plan for agencies with 400 AI Marketer credits a month per client workspace, without a listed price.

## What Profound used to cost

Until around September 2026, reviews and comparison sites reported two self-serve plans:

- **Starter:** $99 a month, ChatGPT only, 50 prompts.
- **Growth:** $399 a month, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, 100 prompts.

If an article still quotes these as current, it was written before the change.

## Is Profound worth it?

For enterprise brands, it can be. Profound covers many engines, keeps all-time history and meets enterprise security requirements. For a startup, the question is whether you can justify a sales-led contract before you know how visible you are. Running a free scan or a trial first gives you a baseline either way.

## FAQ

### How much does Profound cost per month?

Profound does not publish a price for brands as of November 2026. Enterprise is quoted on request, and there is a free 7-day trial.

### Does Profound have a free plan?

There is a free 7-day trial with 50 prompts on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. There is no free plan after the trial.

### Does Profound offer monthly billing?

The pricing page does not say. Enterprise terms are set with sales.
