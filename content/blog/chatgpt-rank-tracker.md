**A ChatGPT rank tracker asks ChatGPT the same buyer questions repeatedly and records how often your brand appears, where in the answer, and whether it is recommended.** There is no fixed "position 1" in ChatGPT: the same question can produce a different list each time. So the useful numbers are rates and averages, not a single rank.

## What to measure instead of a rank

| Metric | What it means |
|---|---|
| Mention rate | Share of answers that name you |
| Recommendation rate | Share of answers that tell the buyer to choose you |
| Average position | Where you appear in lists when you do appear |
| Share of voice | Your mentions versus competitors' on the same questions |
| Citation rate | Share of answers that link to your site |

## Search mode matters

ChatGPT answers some questions from memory and searches the web for others. When it searches, the brands it names come largely from the pages it reads, which change week to week. Track ChatGPT with search, because that is what buyers increasingly see for "best" and "alternatives" questions. See [how ChatGPT decides which brands to recommend](/blog/how-chatgpt-decides-which-brands-to-recommend).

## FAQ

### Can I see my ranking in ChatGPT?

Not as a fixed number. You can see how often you appear and your average position across repeated runs, which is what rank trackers for ChatGPT measure.

### How many times should I ask each question?

At least three runs per question per engine before reading a trend. Weekly tracking smooths out the run-to-run variation.

### Does ChatGPT have analytics for brands?

No. OpenAI does not offer brand analytics, so tracking means asking questions and recording the answers, by hand or with a tool.
