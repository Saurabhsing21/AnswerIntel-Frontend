**The ten AI visibility trackers worth comparing in 2026 are Rankscale, Seenlense, Otterly.AI, LLMrefs, Peec AI, Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit, Ahrefs Brand Radar, Scrunch, AthenaHQ and Profound.** They all do the same core job: ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and other AI assistants the questions your buyers ask, and record whether your brand is named. Entry prices run from $17 a month to custom enterprise contracts, and the cheapest plan is not the best value per prompt.

We opened every vendor's pricing page in a browser on November 14, 2026, took the screenshots below, and recorded each number in a [facts sheet](#how-we-checked). Seenlense is our product. We've kept its section to the same format as the others, and said where a competitor is the better pick.

## What we found when we did the maths

The headline price hides how much tracking you get. We divided each entry plan's price by what it includes, using only numbers printed on the pricing pages.

| Plan | Price | Cost per tracked prompt | Answers a month (max) | Cost per 1,000 answers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LLMrefs All in One | $79 | **$0.16** | Not stated | – |
| Seenlense Starter | $20 | $0.40 | ~645 (50 × 3 engines × 4.3 weeks) | $31 |
| Otterly.AI Standard | $189 | $1.18 | 15,000 credits | **$12.60** |
| Otterly.AI Lite | $29 | $1.45 | 2,000 credits | $14.50 |
| Peec AI Starter | $95 | $1.90 | ~4,500 (50 × 3 × 30 days) | $21.10 |
| Scrunch Core | $250 | $2.00 | 5,000 responses | $50 |
| Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit | $99 | $3.96 | Not stated | – |
| Rankscale Pro | $99 | Unlimited terms | 4,800 | $20.60 |
| AthenaHQ Starter | $295 | Unlimited prompts | 3,600 credits | $81.90 |

Two things stood out:

- **Daily trackers are cheaper per answer, weekly trackers are cheaper per question.** Otterly.AI and Peec AI run every prompt every day, so they collect far more answers for the money. Seenlense and LLMrefs run weekly, so the same budget covers many more questions. If your buyers ask 50 different things, breadth matters more than asking the same 20 questions 30 times.
- **"Unlimited prompts" usually means credit-limited.** Rankscale and AthenaHQ let you add as many prompts as you like, but every answer spends a credit. Work out prompts × engines × runs before you buy.

> **Our take:** most small teams make decisions weekly, and single AI answers vary a lot from run to run. Pay for daily data only if someone will actually look at it daily.

## Rankscale

![Rankscale's plan comparison table showing 120 to 12,000 monthly credits, 17+ AI engines and hourly-to-monthly scheduling](/blog-assets/best-ai-visibility-tools/rankscale-table.jpg "Rankscale's plan table. Essentials (left column) is yearly-only; every plan covers 17+ engines.")

Rankscale is credit-based with the widest engine list we found: 17+ engines on every plan, unlimited search terms, and scheduling from hourly to monthly. Essentials is $17 a month, billed yearly only, for 120 credits (up to 480 answers) and 2 brand dashboards. Pro is $99 a month for 1,200 credits.

- **Pros:** cheapest entry, most engines, flexible schedules, credits roll over on Pro and up.
- **Cons:** Essentials is yearly-only; you have to budget credits per engine and per run.
- **Best for:** people who want many engines and are happy to manage credits.

## Seenlense

![Seenlense pricing: Starter $20 for 50 prompts on 3 AI models, Growth $50 for 120 prompts on 4, Pro $100 for 200 prompts on 7](/blog-assets/best-ai-visibility-tools/seenlense.jpg "Seenlense's plans. Every plan tracks ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Gemini weekly.")

Seenlense (our product) starts at $20 a month for 50 prompts asked weekly on ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Gemini, all three searching the live web. Growth ($50) adds Claude, 120 prompts and before-and-after experiments; Pro ($100) adds DeepSeek, Llama and Mistral, 200 prompts and 10 projects. Seats are unlimited.

What it does differently: it counts **mentions and recommendations separately**, shows which pages each AI cited, and tells you whether a change you shipped moved the numbers beyond normal run-to-run noise ([methodology](/methodology)).

- **Pros:** lowest monthly entry price, recommendation rate, cited sources, experiments, a [free check](/check) without an account.
- **Cons:** weekly only; no Google AI Overviews, AI Mode or Copilot yet; no public API listed.
- **Best for:** founders and small teams who want to know what to fix.

## Common mistakes when choosing

1. **Comparing entry prices only.** Divide by prompts and by answers; the order changes completely.
2. **Paying for daily data nobody reads.** Weekly averages are more reliable than daily snapshots.
3. **Tracking only branded questions.** "What is [your brand]" tells you little. Track "best [category] for [audience]" and "[competitor] alternatives".
4. **Ignoring which engines search the web.** ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini and Google's AI features react to new pages within weeks; models answering from memory don't.
5. **Not checking sources.** The pages an AI cites are your to-do list. Pick a tool that shows them.

## FAQ

### What is the best AI visibility tool?

There isn't one best tool. For startups, Seenlense or Rankscale are the cheapest way to start; for daily data, Otterly.AI or Peec AI; for agencies, LLMrefs; for enterprise, Profound.

### What is the cheapest AI visibility tool?

Rankscale Essentials at $17 a month billed yearly. On monthly billing, Seenlense Starter at $20. Per tracked prompt, LLMrefs is cheapest at about $0.16.

### Do AI visibility tools track Google AI Overviews?

Otterly.AI, Peec AI, Ahrefs Brand Radar, Scrunch, AthenaHQ, LLMrefs and Profound list AI Overviews. Seenlense does not yet.

### Is daily tracking better than weekly?

Not for most teams. AI answers vary between runs, so weekly averages across many prompts usually give a clearer picture than daily snapshots of a few.
