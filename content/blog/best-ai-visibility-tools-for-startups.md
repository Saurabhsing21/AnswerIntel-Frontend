**For a startup, the best AI visibility tools are the ones you can buy by card for under $100 a month: Seenlense ($20), Rankscale ($17 yearly), Otterly.AI ($29), LLMrefs ($79) and Peec AI ($95).** Enterprise tools like Profound, Scrunch and AthenaHQ's paid plans start around $300 a month or need a sales call, which is hard to justify before you know your baseline.

Prices were checked on each vendor's pricing page on November 14, 2026. Seenlense is our product.

## What a startup actually needs

- **A baseline in one afternoon.** Are you named when buyers ask about your category? Which competitors are?
- **The engines your buyers use.** For most B2B products that is ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini first.
- **Weekly is enough.** AI answers vary from run to run, so a small team acts on weekly trends, not daily wiggles.
- **A reason, not just a score.** Which pages did the AI cite? Why was a competitor recommended instead of you?

### Seenlense

Built for founders and small teams. It generates 30 to 50 buyer questions from your category and competitors, asks them every week on three live-search engines, and reports mentions and recommendations separately, the pages each AI cited, and which competitors win each question. Growth ($50) adds Claude, 120 prompts and before-and-after experiments. It does not track Google AI Overviews or Copilot yet. You can run a [free check](/check) without an account first.

### Rankscale

Credit-based with the widest engine list. Cheapest entry if you pay yearly; you need to budget credits per engine.

### Otterly.AI

The cheapest daily tracker, but 20 prompts covers one product and a few comparisons. The next plan is $189.

### LLMrefs

One plan, 500 prompts, 11 engines and geo-targeting in 50+ countries. Good if you sell into many markets.

### Peec AI

The most polished of the five, with daily tracking and unlimited seats. You pay per model: 3 included, extra models from $30 a month.

## How to choose in ten minutes

1. Write down the 10 questions a buyer would ask before choosing you.
2. Run a free check (Seenlense /check, HubSpot's or Mangools' grader) to see if you appear at all.
3. If you never appear, pick the tool that explains why (sources, competitors). If you already appear, pick the one that tracks the engines and frequency you need.

## FAQ

### Do startups need an AI visibility tool?

If buyers research your category in ChatGPT or Perplexity, yes, because those answers are invisible in Google Analytics until someone clicks. A $20 to $30 tool or a free check gives you the baseline.

### Is weekly tracking enough?

For most startups, yes. Single answers vary a lot, so weekly averages are more useful than daily snapshots.

### Which is cheapest?

Rankscale at $17 a month on yearly billing; on monthly billing, Seenlense at $20.
