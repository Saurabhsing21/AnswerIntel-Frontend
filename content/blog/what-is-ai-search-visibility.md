**AI search visibility is how often AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini name your brand when a buyer asks them about your category.** It covers three things: whether you are mentioned, whether you are recommended, and whether your own pages are cited as a source. You measure it by asking the assistants the questions your buyers ask, many times, and counting.

More buyers now start with a question to an assistant instead of a search box. "What's the best CRM for a five-person agency?" used to return ten blue links. Now it returns a short answer with three names. If you are not one of the three, you were never in the running.

## AI visibility vs SEO in one table

| | Classic SEO | AI search visibility |
|---|---|---|
| What the buyer sees | A ranked list of links | A written answer naming a few brands |
| What you win | A click | A mention, a recommendation or a citation |
| Unit of measurement | Rank for a keyword | Share of answers for a buyer question |
| How results vary | Fairly stable day to day | The same question can get different answers each time |
| Main inputs | Your pages and links to them | Your pages, plus what other sites, reviews and forums say about you |

SEO still matters: assistants that search the web start from a search index (ChatGPT search leans on Bing, Gemini and Google AI Overviews on Google). But ranking first for a keyword does not guarantee the assistant will name you.

## The five numbers worth tracking

1. **Mention rate**: the share of answers that name your brand at all.
2. **Recommendation rate**: the share of answers that tell the buyer to choose you. Being listed in passing is not the same as being recommended.
3. **Average position**: when several brands are named, where you appear. First is worth far more than fifth.
4. **Share of voice**: your mentions divided by all brand mentions across the same answers, including competitors.
5. **Citation rate**: how often the assistant links to your own pages as a source, and which competitor pages it cites instead.

## How to measure it yourself

1. **Write 20 to 50 buyer questions.** Use the words customers use, not your marketing words: "best tool for…", "X vs Y", "alternatives to X", "how do I…".
2. **Ask every question on each assistant you care about.** At least ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. Use a logged-out or fresh session so your history doesn't steer the answer.
3. **Ask each question more than once.** Answers vary between runs. Three runs per question gives a much more honest number than one.
4. **Record every answer.** Note who was mentioned, who was recommended, in what order, and which links were cited.
5. **Repeat weekly.** One scan is a snapshot. The trend tells you whether anything you changed worked.

This is tedious by hand: 30 questions × 3 assistants × 3 runs is 270 answers a week. That is the job SeenLens automates.

## What a good score looks like

There is no universal benchmark yet, because results depend heavily on the category and the questions. Useful comparisons are relative:

- **Against your competitors** on the same questions. If a rival is recommended in 40% of answers and you are in 5%, that gap is the goal.
- **Against yourself over time.** A rising recommendation rate after you publish a comparison page is a real signal.
- **Across assistants.** It is common to do well on Perplexity, which searches the live web for most answers, and poorly on ChatGPT without search, which relies more on what the model learned in training.

## Why competitors show up instead of you

Assistants repeat what the web says about a category. Competitors usually win because:

- They appear in "best X tools" roundups and comparison articles that assistants read and cite.
- They are discussed on Reddit, forums and review sites like G2.
- Their own pages state plainly who the product is for, what it costs and how it compares.
- Their name is consistent everywhere, so the assistant is confident it is one entity.

Each of these is fixable. Start with the questions where a competitor is recommended and you are absent, then look at which sources the assistant cited for that answer.

## FAQ

### Is AI search visibility the same as GEO?

They are closely related. Generative engine optimization (GEO), also called answer engine optimization (AEO), is the work you do to improve your AI search visibility. Visibility is the number you measure.

### How often should I measure AI visibility?

Weekly is a good default. Answers change as assistants update their models and as new pages get indexed, and a weekly cadence is frequent enough to see whether a change moved the numbers.

### Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT answers?

Not in the organic answer. Visibility comes from what the assistant reads and learns about your brand, which is why content, reviews and mentions on other sites matter.

### Does ranking on Google mean AI will recommend me?

It helps, because assistants that search the web start from a search index, but it is not enough. Assistants combine several sources and favour brands that many independent sites mention.
