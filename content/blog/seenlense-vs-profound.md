**Profound is an enterprise platform with custom pricing, up to 10 answer engines, all-time history, API and SSO. Seenlense is a self-serve tracker from $20 a month for founders and small teams.** They rarely compete for the same buyer: if you need SOC 2, SSO and a contract, choose Profound; if you need a baseline and fixes this week on a startup budget, choose Seenlense. Seenlense is our product; Profound details come from its pricing page, checked November 14, 2026.

## Who each is for

- **Profound:** enterprise brands with procurement, security reviews and a dedicated team.
- **Seenlense:** founders and small teams who want to see who AI recommends instead of them and test fixes, without a sales call.

## FAQ

### Is Seenlense cheaper than Profound?

Yes. Seenlense starts at $20 a month by card; Profound shows only a trial and custom Enterprise pricing for brands.

### Does Profound have a self-serve plan?

For brands, not any more: its pricing page lists a 7-day trial and Enterprise. Agencies have a self-serve Agency Growth plan without a listed price.
